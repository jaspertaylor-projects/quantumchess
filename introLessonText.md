# Intro Lesson 

1. Start -- Every piece begins as every piece. Follow the glow and play e2 to e4.
2. After e2-e4 -- Your piece on the e-file claimed the center. This move could have been made by a pawn, rook or queen.  So it has changed to reflect its true nature.
3. After g8-f6.  My g8 piece leapt to f6 became a night and then and then made contact with your piece on e4.  When a piece makes contact with an enemy piece it removes the strongest possible peiece from that pieces possibilities.  We call this kind of contact a weak measuerment (Or a Zap). 
4. After f1-c4.   Only a queen or bishop could make that move.   A mulitple possibility piece only can make contact with its least valuable option.  Notice f7 was weakly measured, but c7 attackable by a queen was not.
5. After b8-c6.  I developed my other knight to c6. A chess sset only has two possible knights.   A conserved quantity.  Since I have already revealed both of my nights it means that none of my other pieces can be a knight.  You can see that reflected in their representation.
6. After d2-d4. Your d-pawn took space in the center. It made no contact yet—but your next move will support it
7. After d7-d5. I pushed d7 to d5 making contact with and zapping two pieces at once.
8. After b2-b3. Your b3 piece makes contact with your own piece on c4, a weak entanglement (Or Heal)! This restores the least valuable missing option to the pieces in contact.  In this case a pawn! 
9.  After c6-d4.  I use my knight to make a hard measurement (Or Take) your piece on d4.  When a piece wiht multiple possibilities is taken it collapses into its weakest possible option.  In thsi cas a pawn.  Look at it in my captured pieces tray.   
10.  After f2-d4.  AAARGH you recaptured my knight, I should have ssen that coming.
11. After c7-c6.  Since my two nights are accounted for my heal spills over into the next least valuable piece a bishop!   
12.  After e4-d5.  A capture and a zap,   I see you are getting the hang of this!
13.  After f8-h8.  Time to castle.  you can castle any two pieces on your back rank that both have rook and king as possible option, but use it wisely you can only do this once per game.
14. After d5-c6.   Watch out here comes a surprise move!
15. After d8-d6.  That's right my piece can still be a pawn,  it took the creation of quantum chess to reveal the true nature of pawns, any pawn in the first two home ranks is able ot move two spaces forward. You don't learn this in classical chess due to the initial condition (starting position).
16. After c6-d7.  A counter surprise!! En Passant  I never see that coming.
17. After e7-e1. I've infiltrated your back rank!  
18. After d7-c8.  A promotion.  Whatever piece this becomes wil;l not count against your piece limit.  it will carry that bar beneath it for the rest of its life.
19. After e8-e2.  Oops, That move really collapsed my options. The solver will automatically change the possibilities of your pieces so that you will have exactly a full chess set when the dust settles.
20. After d4-c5.  Your piece on f8 blocked the zap with a shield icon.   This is because an opponents zap cannot cause spillover collapse into other pieces. In this cas if either a rook or king was eliminated from the possibilities of the piece on f7 it would have had far reaching consequences across my army.  You have to make a capture if you want to do that.
