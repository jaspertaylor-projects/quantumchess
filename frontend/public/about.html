About Quantum Chess

Every piece is every piece — until it moves.

Quantum Chess is a free browser game that reimagines chess through the lens of quantum mechanics. It is not a chess set so much as thirty-two unresolved questions: every piece begins as a superposition of all six classical types — Pawn, Knight, Bishop, Rook, Queen, and King — and only becomes something definite as the game forces it to. Nobody knows which piece is your King at the start. Not your opponent. Not you.

How it works

Moving is measuring

A piece may move like any type it might still be. But the move itself is evidence: afterward, the piece keeps only the identities that could have made that move. A knight’s jump collapses a piece to a definite Knight forever; a long diagonal slide narrows it to Bishop-or-Queen. Choosing how to move is choosing how much of a piece’s identity to spend.

Measurement pulses and decoherence

When your piece finishes a move, it performs a soft measurement on every enemy piece it could capture from its new square. Those pieces are marked, and their “coherence” erodes over the following turns — eventually shedding their least valuable possible identity. Aggression is how you gather information; passivity earns none.

Recoherence

A piece narrowed down to two or fewer possibilities slowly diffuses back toward superposition if left unobserved — a nod to the quantum Zeno effect, where constant observation freezes a system in place and inattention lets it evolve.

Entangled castling

Castling can be performed between any two of your unmoved pieces whose possibilities still include both Rook and King. They collapse into an anti-correlated entangled pair: in every consistent world, exactly one is the King and the other the Rook. Resolve one, and the other instantly becomes its complement — no matter where it stands on the board.

Quantum promotion and conservation

Promotion is a branch, not an assertion: in the worlds where a piece was a Pawn, it becomes a heavy piece funded by a pawn slot; in the others, nothing happened. Throughout, a strict conservation law holds — every side always accounts for exactly its original sixteen pieces across all possible worlds. Normal chess is simply one arrangement this game can collapse into.

Ways to play

  • Versus AI — twelve bots across easy, medium, and hard, each a playful mashup of a physicist and a chess legend, with distinct styles and ratings.
  • Local two-player — hotseat on one device.
  • Online 1v1 — real-time matches against another player.
  • Accounts (optional) — earn a rating and keep a history of your recent games.

New players get a built-in interactive tutorial that walks each rule through with playable diagrams, alongside a full rulebook — both reachable from inside the game.

How it was built

Quantum Chess is an independent project built by one developer. The game engine — superposition, collapse, the conservation solver, measurement pulses, and the AI — runs entirely in your browser; a small server handles only online matchmaking and move relay. The rules are fully deterministic: despite the quantum theming, there is no randomness in how positions resolve.
