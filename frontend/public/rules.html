Quantum Chess: quick rules

Every piece starts with all six identities. There is no randomness: your moves determine what each piece can be.

Move, then make contact

Move as any identity your piece still holds. It keeps only the identities that can make that move. From its new square it contacts pieces using its cheapest remaining identity: Pawn, Knight, Bishop, Rook, Queen, King.

Zap enemies; heal friends

Enemy contacts try to remove the strongest identity, King first. Zaps resolve jointly and can cause distant census collapses. The engine keeps the largest safe set of removals, preferring stronger identities, then earlier algebraic squares. It protects the final King possibility by retrying below King.

Friendly contacts jointly restore feasible missing identities, normally cheapest first. A kingless team tries King first. Pawn cannot return to promoted pieces or pieces on their promotion rank. Red means zap, green means heal, and gold means a shield: no safe removal was available.

Why distant pieces collapse

Each team must fit one chess set: 8 Pawns, 2 Knights, 2 Bishops, 2 Rooks, 1 Queen and 1 King. Captured pieces still count. When identities become claimed, the census removes impossible identities from teammates anywhere on the board. A closed group can collapse from a zap; it is not automatically shielded.

Captures and special moves

A captured piece resolves as its cheapest remaining identity. Once per game, castle two back-rank superpositions that both include Rook and King, with a clear gap between them. Possible pawns can double-step from either of their first two home ranks; en passant is available on the next turn. Promotion replaces a Pawn branch with Knight, Bishop, Rook and Queen branches while retaining its Pawn census slot.

Win by checkmate

Only a revealed King is subject to check. You cannot leave it capturable. Checkmate wins; erasing King possibilities does not. Stalemate, threefold repetition, and 100 half-moves without a capture, definite Pawn move, promotion or net identity loss draw the game.
